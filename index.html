Why trades businesses miss out on local search

You're not in the map pack

Most local enquiries come from the three Google Maps results at the top of the page. An incomplete or unverified Google Business Profile keeps you out of them.

Your website doesn't say where you work

If your site never mentions Poole, Christchurch or Wimborne, Google has no reason to show you to customers there.

Reviews and listings are patchy

Inconsistent names, numbers and addresses across directories, and too few recent reviews, quietly drag your rankings down.

Common questions

How long does local SEO take to work?

Fixes to your Google Business Profile can show results within a few weeks. Ranking your website for competitive searches such as 'plumber Bournemouth' usually takes three to six months of steady work.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. Most trades websites can be fixed. I'll tell you honestly if yours is holding you back and whether a rebuild is worth the money.

Are there long contracts?

No. The fix-up and setup are one-off fixed prices. The monthly retainer runs month to month and you can stop with 30 days' notice.

My focus is Dorset and the BCP area because I know the local market, but I'm happy to talk if you're just over the border in Hampshire, Wiltshire or Somerset.
