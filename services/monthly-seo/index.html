Common questions

What exactly do I get each month?

It depends on your business. Every month includes a mix of new location and service pages, on-page optimisation, technical SEO checks and fixes, Google Business Profile optimisation, ranking tracking and a monthly report. The exact mix is tailored to your goals and agreed with you upfront.

How long until I see results?

SEO builds over time. The first month goes on fixes and foundations, months two and three add new pages and Google Business Profile activity, and that's usually when early movement shows. Most clients start to see meaningful results in rankings and calls after around 3 months. I can't guarantee specific rankings, and anyone who does is guessing.

Is there a minimum term?

No. The retainer is month to month with 30 days' notice, so you can cancel any time. I'd recommend giving it at least 3 months, because local SEO builds over time, but that's your call.

How will I know it's working?

Every month you get a one-page report covering calls, direction requests and enquiries from Google, where you rank for your main searches, and what I did.
