Common questions

What makes a website "SEO-ready"?

It's built with what Google needs from day one: fast, clean code, a mobile-first layout, LocalBusiness schema, a unique title and description on every page, a sitemap, and separate pages for your services and the towns you cover. It's the same standard this website is built to.

Can I change the text and photos myself?

Yes. Every website comes with a simple login editor, so you can update text and swap photos without touching any code. If you'd rather not, the optional care plan covers edits for you.

Where is the website hosted, and who owns it?

I set it up on free or low-cost hosting with no lock-in. The domain is registered in your name and you own the code, so you can move it or take it elsewhere whenever you like.

Do I have to take the care plan?

No. The care plan is optional at £30 a month and covers edits, uptime checks and regular SEO checks. Your website works perfectly well without it.

Can you build it in WordPress instead?

Yes, a WordPress version is available on request and priced on quote. WordPress needs paid hosting and regular updates, so it usually costs more to run than the standard build.

Will a new website get me to the top of Google straight away?

No one can honestly promise that. A well-built website gives you the right foundations, but your Google Business Profile, reviews and local listings matter just as much. That's why many clients pair a new site with local SEO setup.
