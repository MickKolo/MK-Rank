Common questions

I don't have a business address. Can I still appear on Google Maps?

Yes. Most trades are service-area businesses. Your Google Business Profile can hide your home address and show the towns you cover instead.

Can you get me more reviews?

I'll set up a simple way to ask happy customers for a review by text or email, with a short link and wording you can reuse. I never buy or fake reviews, because that breaks Google's rules and risks your listing.

Which directories do you use?

The ones that matter for UK trades, such as Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yell, Thomson Local, Checkatrade, Trustatrade, MyBuilder and relevant trade bodies. I'll agree the list with you first.
