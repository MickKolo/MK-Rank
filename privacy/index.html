Privacy notice

This notice explains how MK Rank ("I", "me"), run by Mick Kolodziej, uses personal data sent through this website. Contact: [EMAIL].

What I collect

When you use the contact form I receive the details you enter: your name, business name, trade, email, phone number, website and message. When you book a call, the booking service collects your name, email and chosen time.

Why and on what basis

I use these details only to reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to deliver the service. The lawful basis is legitimate interests, or steps taken at your request before entering a contract.

Who processes it

Form messages are delivered by Formspree and call bookings are handled by Calendly. Both act as data processors. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. I don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as UK law requires.

Cookies

This website doesn't set tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing me. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
