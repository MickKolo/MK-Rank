Privacy notice

This notice explains how MK Rank ("I", "me"), run by Mick, uses personal data sent through this website or by phone. Contact: 07469 886043.

What I collect

When you call or text me I receive your phone number and anything you tell me about your business. If you use a contact form or online booking on this site, I receive the details you enter, such as your name, business name, trade, email, phone number, website and message.

Why and on what basis

I use these details only to reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to deliver the service. The lawful basis is legitimate interests, or steps taken at your request before entering a contract.

Who processes it

Any messages sent through a contact form on this site are delivered by Formspree, and any online call bookings are handled by Calendly. Both act as data processors. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. I don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as UK law requires.

Cookies

This website doesn't set tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by contacting me. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
