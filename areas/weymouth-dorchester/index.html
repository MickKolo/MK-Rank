Why local SEO works differently in South Dorset

Fewer people search here than in Bournemouth or Poole, but the competition is thinner too, so a well-set-up trades business can rank quickly. The challenge is distance: Google favours businesses close to the person searching, and your customers may be 20 miles apart.

  • Service-area settings that list each town you cover, from Weymouth and Dorchester to Bridport and Wareham.
  • Area pages for the places you want work from, with real local detail rather than copy-and-paste text.
  • Reviews that mention where the job was, which help you appear in villages further from your base.
  • Local links and mentions from Dorset suppliers, parish newsletters, clubs you sponsor and trade bodies.
