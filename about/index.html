About Mick Kolodziej, local SEO consultant in Dorset

I'm Mick, and I run MK Rank. I help plumbers, electricians, builders and other trades across Dorset get more work from Google.

Five years in digital marketing, now focused on local trades

I've spent five years in digital marketing, and I now specialise in SEO, focusing on local search for trades businesses.

That broader marketing background is a strength. I understand where leads actually come from, how ads and content work alongside search, and what makes the phone ring, not just where you rank.

Along the way I noticed the same thing again and again. Trades businesses do great work and have loyal customers, but they lose jobs online to competitors who simply set up their Google listing better.

So I started MK Rank to offer the kind of SEO small trades businesses actually need: practical work, fixed prices and no long contracts. You won't get a 40-page report full of jargon. You'll get the work done and a clear explanation of what changed.

How I work

  • Directly. You deal with me from the first call to the monthly report.
  • Honestly. No guaranteed number-one rankings, no fake reviews and no shortcuts that put your listing at risk.
  • Locally. I'm based in Dorset and know the local market, from the BCP towns to the rural west.
  • Practising what I preach. This website is built to the same SEO standards I use for clients: fast, mobile-first and accessible.
